About Me

My name is Akintayo Ibrahim Atanda. I am a Computer Science student at Kwara State University (KWASU), Malete, Nigeria, with a strong interest in web development, software engineering, and technology.

My journey into technology started with an interest in understanding how websites and computer applications work. I am currently developing my programming skills and learning how to turn ideas into functional, user-friendly websites.

I am learning frontend development using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I also practise using Git and GitHub to manage my code and publish my projects online. I enjoy learning new things, solving problems, correcting errors, and improving my coding skills through practice.

As part of my learning journey, I have worked on personal projects such as Student Hub, a platform designed to help students organise academic information, and AttendCheck, a project focused on attendance management. I am also developing DailyMate, a student productivity application, and Atanova AI, an AI chatbot project. These projects help me apply what I learn and gain practical experience in software development.

I believe that becoming a good programmer requires consistency, patience, creativity, and a willingness to learn from mistakes. Whenever I encounter a coding problem, I try to understand the cause and find a solution instead of giving up.

My goal is to become a skilled software engineer, build useful digital solutions, contribute to meaningful technology projects, and continue growing as a developer. I am open to learning opportunities, collaborations, and projects that will help me gain more experience in the technology industry.
