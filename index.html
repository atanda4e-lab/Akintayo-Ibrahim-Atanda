About Me

## About Me My name is Akintayo Ibrahim Atanda, and I am a Computer Science student at Kwara State University (KWASU), Malete, Nigeria. I am an aspiring software developer with a strong interest in frontend development, web technologies, and creating digital solutions that solve real-world problems. My journey into technology is driven by curiosity, creativity, and a desire to understand how software works. I am developing my programming skills through practical experience, personal projects, and continuous learning. My current focus is on HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive web design, and building interactive websites that provide useful experiences for users. As part of my development journey, I have worked on projects such as Atanda Student Hub, a student-focused academic platform; AttendCheck, an attendance management application; DailyMate, a student productivity application; and Atanova AI, an AI chatbot project. These projects allow me to explore different areas of software development while improving my problem-solving abilities and technical knowledge. I believe that becoming a good developer requires patience, consistency, creativity, and a willingness to learn from mistakes. Every project gives me an opportunity to improve my coding practices, explore new ideas, and turn concepts into working applications. Beyond writing code, I am interested in technology innovation, software engineering, and using digital tools to make everyday activities easier and more efficient. My long-term ambition is to become a skilled computer scientist and software engineer, build impactful technology products, collaborate with other developers, and contribute to the growth of the technology community. I am always looking forward to learning new technologies, connecting with like-minded people, collaborating on meaningful projects, and discovering new opportunities to grow in the technology industry.
