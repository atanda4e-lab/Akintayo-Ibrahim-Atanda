About Me

My name is Akintayo Ibrahim Atanda. I am a Computer Science student at Kwara State University (KWASU), Malete, Nigeria, with a strong interest in web development, software engineering, and technology.

My journey into technology started with an interest in understanding how websites and computer applications work. I am developing my programming skills and learning how to turn ideas into functional, user-friendly websites.

I am learning frontend development using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I also practise using Git and GitHub to manage my code and publish my projects online. I enjoy solving problems, correcting errors, and improving my skills through regular practice.

I have worked on personal projects such as Student Hub, a platform designed to help students organise academic information, and AttendCheck, a project focused on attendance management. I am also developing DailyMate, a student productivity application, and Atanova AI, an AI chatbot project.

These projects help me apply what I learn, practise programming, and gain practical experience in software development.

My goal is to become a skilled software engineer, build useful digital solutions, contribute to meaningful technology projects, and continue growing as a developer. I am open to learning opportunities, collaboration, and projects that will help me gain more experience in the technology industry.
